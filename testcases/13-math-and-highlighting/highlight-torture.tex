\section{Inline math edge cases}
Price is \$5 and \$10 --- escaped dollars must NOT start math colouring.
Real inline math $a + b$ then text, then $c$ again; the text between must be default colour.
A dollar inside a comment: % this $ must not open math
And this line after the comment must be normal text colour.
Math with an escaped dollar inside: $\text{cost} = \$3$ still ends at the last dollar.
Empty math $$ $$ on one line, then \( inline paren math \) and text.

\section{Display math}
\[
  \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}
\]
$$ \sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6} $$
\begin{align*}
  f(x) &= x^2 \\
       &= x \cdot x % comment inside math: the % part is comment colour, the rest math
\end{align*}
\begin{equation}
  E = mc^2 \label{eq:emc}
\end{equation}
Text after the equation must be default colour again, and \eqref{eq:emc} is a command.

\section{Verbatim and comments}

$ this $ \is \verbatim % not a comment
\end{verbatim}
\begin{lstlisting}[language=Python]

\end{lstlisting}

mis-coloured. Record what you see.
