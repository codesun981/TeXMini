Modern pipeline: pdflatex -> biber -> pdflatex -> pdflatex. latexmk detects
\texttt{.bcf} and runs biber automatically.

We cite \textcite{knuth1984}, \parencite{lamport1994} and
\autocite{goossens1994}.

After the first successful build, run Clean and Rebuild (Opt-Cmd-B): the \texttt{.bbl},
\texttt{.bcf} and \texttt{.run.xml} files are deleted and the bibliography must
come back intact.
